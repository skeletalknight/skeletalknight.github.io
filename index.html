Jinyuan Mao 茅晋源

I am a senior undergraduate at Zhejiang University, pursuing a dual degree in Statistics and Information Management & Information Systems. My research is on generative AI and efficient AI. I am interested in making video diffusion models and interactive world models faster, more consistent over long horizons, and easier to steer.

Most recently, I was a research intern at PixAI, working with Chenxi Liu (University of Victoria) on interactive image generation. Before that, I spent eight months at the ZIP Lab advised by Prof. Bohan Zhuang, working on sparse attention and KV-cache design for long video generation.

I also enjoy high-performance computing: our team placed 1st globally at the SC25 IndySCC, and I look after the GPU infrastructure at ZJU X-Lab.

NeurIPS 2026

WorldAttention: An Efficient Attention Architecture for Interactive Video World Models

Zeyu Zhang*, Jinyuan Mao*, Dakai An*, Wangbo Zhao, Hanfeng Lu, Jiasheng Tang, Yinghao Yu, Wei Wang, Bohan Zhuang

NeurIPS 2026 Reviews: Accept / Accept / Accept

Attention heads in video diffusion transformers are sparse in very different ways. Hybrid Sparse Attention pairs a linear global branch with head-adaptive block-sparse attention for a 2.21× end-to-end speedup at matched quality.

Eurographics 2027

HimmBO: High-Dimensional Interactive Model-Merging Bayesian Optimization for Image Generation

Jinyuan Mao, Zhihao Shen, Chenxi Liu

Under review at Eurographics 2027

Two-stage preferential Bayesian optimization scales interactive LoRA merging from a few dozen to 1,080 adapters. A style-aware kernel shares preferences across similar adapters, so it needs under 1/10 of the image generations used by prior work.
